Care

Hygiene and grooming are the base of presence. Small, daily, non-negotiable.

Grooming schedule

Morning routine

Glass of water first
Brush teeth, scrape tongue
Cold shower: face, underarms, feet
Moisturiser and sunscreen
Deodorant, fragrance, clean fitted clothes
Comb hair, check posture in the mirror

Night routine

Brush and floss
Face wash and moisturiser
Tidy or trim if it is due
Lay out tomorrow's clothes
Phone away, lights out before 11

Standards

Haircut every 3 to 4 weeks, nails weekly, clean shoes, ironed clothes, fresh bedsheets weekly. Smell clean, look sharp, stand tall. Presence starts with maintenance. If skin or hair problems persist, see a doctor.
